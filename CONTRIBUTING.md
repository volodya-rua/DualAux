# Contributing

Thanks for helping. Issues and pull requests are welcome.

- Keep it small and simple. Minimal code, minimal UI, no new dependencies unless they clearly earn their place.
- **Privacy stays absolute:** no analytics, telemetry, network calls or data collection. A change that adds any of these will not be merged.
- Test on the platform you changed and say what you tested. Several backends (Linux, macOS, Android) are still untested on real hardware, so reports from them are very useful.
- By submitting a contribution you agree it is licensed under the project's [MIT License](LICENSE) and that you have the right to submit it.
