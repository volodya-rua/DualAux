- **Hearing safety.** Playing audio on several headphones at once can be loud. Start with the volume low and raise it slowly. Prolonged loud listening can permanently damage hearing. You are responsible for your listening levels and for anyone you share audio with.
- **Content rights.** DualAux only sends your device's own audio to your own outputs. It does not copy, store or share content, and it does not remove copy protection. Android apps that block audio capture (for example some DRM video apps) stay blocked. Use it only with content you have the right to play.
- **Hardware.** Results depend on your devices, drivers and operating system. Wireless devices add delay, so outputs may not stay perfectly in sync. System changes made by the app (a temporary combined output on Linux and macOS, the default device on those systems) are undone when you press Stop, but a crash may leave them in place.
